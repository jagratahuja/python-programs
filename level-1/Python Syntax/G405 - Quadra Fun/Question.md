# Program G405

## Description
This program applies arithmetic operations to solve real-world problems involving geometry and cost calculation.  
It calculates the perimeter of a rectangular field for fencing, the area of a smaller rectangular pitch for turf installation, and computes worker wages based on hourly rates and working hours.

## Tasks Included
- Task 1: Calculate the perimeter of a rectangular sports field and the area of a hockey pitch.
- Task 2: Calculate the wages for workers based on hourly rate and total working hours, and compute the total payment.

## Concepts Practiced
- Perimeter of a rectangle (`2 × (length + width)`)
- Area of a rectangle (`length × width`)
- Arithmetic calculations
- Real-world problem solving
- Working with float values
